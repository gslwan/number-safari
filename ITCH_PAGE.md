# Number Safari

A playful multiplication adventure. Guide a growing lion through number grids,
practice times tables 2–12, and explore three game modes.

## Play

Choose a times table and help your lion collect its multiples. Correct answers
grow your lion and its mane; mistakes shrink it by one growth step.

- **Beginner:** Follow a path with one correct adjacent choice at each step.
- **Classic:** Explore the grid and safely revisit cleared tiles.
- **Freestyle:** Move freely and choose when to eat.

Use **arrow keys or WASD** to move. In Freestyle, press **Space or Enter** to eat.
Press **Esc** to return to the menu. Click inside the game to focus the keyboard.
A desktop browser and keyboard are required. Click to play to enable sound.

Completed rounds are stored locally in your browser when browser storage is
available. Clearing site data clears this history.
